## Method

- Strategy: Moving Average Crossover (20/50), risk layer off, on the synthetic `data/sample_data.csv`
- Each timing covers one `Backtester.run()`: reading the CSV, the bar-by-bar loop, and the metrics
- 5 runs per case, one after another in one process; the table shows the median, with the fastest and slowest run

## Results

| Case | Bars (days) | Rows (bars x symbols) | Median | Min | Max | Median per bar |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 symbols, 1 year | 262 | 1,310 | 0.49 s | 0.48 s | 0.50 s | 1.87 ms |
| 5 symbols, 5 years | 1,305 | 6,525 | 3.46 s | 3.34 s | 3.75 s | 2.65 ms |
| 25 symbols, 5 years (full sample file) | 1,305 | 32,625 | 26.72 s | 26.53 s | 27.22 s | 20.47 ms |

Why time per bar is not constant: on every bar the engine filters the loaded rows up to that date, and the strategy recomputes each symbol's indicators over that symbol's full history. More symbols or a longer history means more work per bar. Computing indicators once per symbol up front would avoid most of that repeated work.
